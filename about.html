Experience

A teacher of surgery, now operating full-time

Dr. Rajavardan specialised in veterinary surgery and radiology for his master's degree. He then taught surgery to veterinary students at the Teaching Veterinary Hospital in Korutla, which gave him years of hands-on work on hundreds of clinical cases.

He opened West Paws in February 2025 as a small animal surgical centre for Manikonda, Narsingi and Puppalaguda. Pet parents get the same standard of surgery and diagnostics as at a teaching hospital, along with time and attention from the doctor himself. Other hospitals in the city also call him in as a consultant surgeon.

Surgical expertise

Procedures he performs regularly

All of these come from cases treated and documented in the last year and a half.

Orthopaedics

Fracture fixation (IM pins, cerclage wire, cross pinning with K-wires), FHO for hip dysplasia and femoral head fractures, and amputations.

Soft tissue & oncology

Removal of mammary, mast cell, perianal, nasal, testicular and digital tumours, with histopathology to guide follow-up care.

Abdominal emergencies

GDV (bloat), intestinal and linear foreign bodies, exploratory laparotomy, gastrotomy and enterotomy.

Urinary surgery

Perineal urethrostomy and its revision, cystotomy for bladder and urethral stones.

Reproductive surgery

Spay and neuter, pyometra, en-bloc removal of the gravid uterus, and cryptorchid surgery.

Eye & perineal surgery

Cherry eye correction (pocket technique), enucleation, and perineal hernia repair with mesh.
