Facilities

14 services under one roof

General health check-ups

Head-to-tail examinations, weight and diet advice, and senior pet screening.

Vaccination

Puppy and kitten schedules, annual boosters and anti-rabies vaccination, with digital reminders.

Deworming & parasite control

Deworming plans and tick and flea prevention suited to your pet's age and lifestyle.

General & soft tissue surgery

Spay and neuter, tumour removal, hernia repair, pyometra, C-section and wound reconstruction.

Orthopaedic surgery

Fracture fixation with pins, wires and cross-pinning, FHO for hip dysplasia, and amputations.

Digital X-ray

In-house digital radiography for fractures, foreign bodies, the chest and the abdomen.

Ultrasound

Abdominal and pregnancy ultrasound for fast, non-invasive diagnosis.

ECG

Heart rhythm checks, including pre-anaesthetic screening for older pets.

Blood & biochemical tests

Complete blood count and organ-function panels to support diagnosis and safer anaesthesia.

Critical care unit

Monitoring, IV fluids and oxygen support for emergencies, after surgery and for very sick pets.

Dental care

Scaling, polishing and extractions to treat bad breath and gum disease.

Pregnancy diagnosis

Pregnancy confirmation and monitoring by ultrasound, plus whelping advice.

Grooming & bathing

Medicated and regular baths, nail trims and ear cleaning.

Pet food & accessories

Prescription and everyday diets, leashes, cones, beds and other essentials in store.

Surgery at West Paws

A proper operation theatre, not a back room

Our dedicated operation theatre is set up for sterile procedures, with anaesthetic monitoring and post-operative care in the critical care unit.

  • Pre-anaesthetic blood work and ECG when needed
  • Digital X-ray before and after orthopaedic surgery
  • Histopathology for removed tumours
  • Clear written aftercare and follow-up reviews
