How to reach West Paws

We are on Narsingi–Puppalaguda Main Road in Alkapur Township, close to Manikonda, Narsingi, Puppalaguda, Kokapet and Neknampur.

By road

  1. Get onto Narsingi–Puppalaguda Main Road. It runs between Narsingi and Puppalaguda/Manikonda.
  2. Look for Geethanjali Vedika School, opposite D-Mart Ready, near the high-tension power line in Alkapur Township.
  3. Turn into the lane at the school. West Paws is a short way down the lane, on your left.
  4. Coming from Yoga Circle? Head east past Ganesh Mandapam, turn right towards Nethaji Park, then turn right again at the end. West Paws will be on your right.
